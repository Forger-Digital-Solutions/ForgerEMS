# Donation Transparency — Forger Digital Solutions

Donations, if public support platforms are configured, are voluntary support for independent development. They do not purchase software ownership, guaranteed features, priority support, investment returns, equity, profit sharing, or repayment.

Funding updates may be published when practical. Project priorities may change with safety work, user feedback, compatibility findings, and available resources. ForgerEMS should not store supporter payment information; donation platforms process payments under their own policies.

Forger Digital Solutions will not request cryptocurrency wallet seed phrases, remote-access credentials, passwords, API keys, or sensitive banking credentials as part of a donation. No tax-deductibility claim is made; donors should consult their own tax professional.
